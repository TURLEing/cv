*Co-first author (listed first).* Accepted at COLM 2026. Introduces the first benchmark for evaluating Deep AI Research Systems (DARS) on frontier AI research: 65 questions from authentic research settings spanning 35 AI subjects and 3 task types, paired with expert-rubric insight evaluation and citation faithfulness/groundedness assessment. Leading DARS reached 69–70% insight coverage, outperforming other DARS by 20–30 points, while exposing an open-consulting strength and a high-faithfulness/low-groundedness pattern. Dataset and code are released.
