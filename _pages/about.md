I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Electronic Information (Artificial Intelligence) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by [Prof. Pengfei Liu](https://plms.ai/). My research focuses on large language models: evaluation of deep AI research systems, reinforcement learning for instruction following, and fine-grained reward modeling.

I have first-author / co-first-author papers accepted at COLM 2026 (*Rubrics to Tokens*; *ResearcherBench*). Previously, I interned at Alibaba (Taobao & Tmall Group) on agent reinforcement learning.
