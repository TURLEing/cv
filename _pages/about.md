I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Electronic Information (Artificial Intelligence) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by [Prof. Pengfei Liu](https://plms.ai/). My research interests lie in LLM evaluation and fine-grained post-training. Previously, I interned at Alibaba (Taobao & Tmall Group) on agent reinforcement learning.
