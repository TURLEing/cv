I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Electronic Information (Artificial Intelligence) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Pengfei Liu. I received my B.S. in E-Commerce and Law from the International School of Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, graduating with a GPA of 91.7/100 (Rank 1/165) and the National Scholarship.

My research focuses on large language models: evaluation of deep AI research systems, reinforcement learning for instruction following, and fine-grained reward modeling. I have first-author / co-first-author papers accepted at COLM 2026 (Rubrics to Tokens; ResearcherBench). I previously interned at Alibaba (Taobao & Tmall Group) on agent reinforcement learning and at Lingxin Intelligence on the CharacterGLM project.
